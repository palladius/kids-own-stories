# Product Definition — Kids' Own Stories (`kids-own-stories`)

## Vision
An interactive 3D open-book web showcase (`https://palladius.github.io/kids-own-stories/`) and deterministic archive (`stories.yaml`) for original stories invented at the microphone by Riccardo's sons **Alessandro ("Ale", 2018)** and **Sebastian ("Seby", 2020)**.

## Core Pillars
1. **3D Open-Book Zero-Scroll Experience (`100dvh`)**: Full-viewport double-page book carousel with Pixar 3D illustrations on the left page and cohesive fairy-tale paragraphs (`cantastorie` IT & EN) on the right page.
2. **Bilingual Narrated Audiobooks**: Calm female narration (`Aoede` via Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS) with soft background piano in both Italian (`🇮🇹 IT`) and English (`🇬🇧 EN`).
3. **Preserved Raw Child Authenticity**: Word-for-word transcripts (`Sbobinatura Fedele`) and original microphone recordings (`audio_originale.mp3`/`.ogg`) tucked discreetly into a behind-the-scenes modal at the bottom of the page.
4. **Interactive Story Intake & Regeneration**: In-app tools to request story regeneration with custom creative prompts ("La storia non mi piace, rigenerala") and to record new microphone stories directly from the browser into GCP storage.
